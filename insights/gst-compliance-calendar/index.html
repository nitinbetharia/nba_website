GST Compliance Calendar 2024

By N. Betharia & Associates · 4 August 2025

Monthly, quarterly, and annual GST return due dates and special compliance reminders.

Important disclaimer

This compliance calendar is for general reference only. Due dates may vary by business type, turnover, and notifications. Based on the CGST Act, 2017 and notifications as of 2024. Verify current due dates and consult a qualified GST practitioner before acting. N. Betharia & Associates does not assume responsibility for penalties arising from reliance solely on this calendar.

Monthly Compliance Requirements

GSTR-1 — 11th of next month

Applicability: Taxpayers with turnover > ₹5 Crores

  • Details of outward supplies
  • Export and import invoices
  • Credit/Debit notes

GSTR-1 — 13th of next month

Applicability: Taxpayers with turnover ≤ ₹5 Crores

  • Same outward supply reporting for smaller taxpayers
  • Can file quarterly if opted

GSTR-3B Filing & Payment — 20th of next month

Applicability: All registered taxpayers

  • Summary return with tax liability
  • Input Tax Credit availment
  • Tax payment along with return

GSTR-7 (TDS) — 10th of next month

Applicability: TDS deductors (government entities and specified persons under Section 51)

GSTR-8 (TCS) — 10th of next month

Applicability: TCS collectors (e-commerce operators and other notified collectors)

GSTR-6 (ISD) — 10th of next month

Applicability: Input Service Distributors — distribution of ITC from head office to branches

Quarterly Compliance

GSTR-1 (Quarterly) — 18th of month following quarter

Applicability: Taxpayers with turnover ≤ ₹5 Crores (if opted)

  • April–June: Due 18th July
  • July–Sep: Due 18th October
  • Oct–Dec: Due 18th January
  • Jan–Mar: Due 18th April

GSTR-4 (Composition) — 30th of month following quarter

Applicability: Composition dealers — quarterly return under the simplified mechanism

Annual Compliance

GSTR-9 (Annual Return) — 31st December

Applicability: Taxpayers with turnover > ₹2 Crores — comprehensive annual return reconciled with books

GSTR-9C (Reconciliation) — 31st December

Applicability: Taxpayers with turnover > ₹5 Crores — audited reconciliation with CA/CMA certification

GSTR-4 (Annual) — 30th April

Applicability: Composition dealers — annual return following the financial year

Special Compliance

Compliance Due Date Applicability
Monthly TDS/TCS Payment 20th of next month Deductors/Collectors
E-way Bill Generation Before goods movement Consignments > ₹50,000
E-invoice Generation At the time of sale B2B sales for notified turnover thresholds
ITC Reversal September return Blocked credits as per Section 17(5)
