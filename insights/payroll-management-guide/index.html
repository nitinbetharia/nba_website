Payroll Management Guide 2024

By N. Betharia & Associates · 4 August 2025

Salary components, PF/ESI/TDS calculations, processing calendar, and compliance reminders.

Comprehensive guide for salary processing and compliance.

Important disclaimer

This payroll guide is for general reference and educational purposes only. Calculations and compliance requirements vary by state laws, industry rules, and company policies. Based on labor laws, Income Tax Act provisions, and EPF/ESI regulations as applicable in 2024. Consult qualified HR professionals and Chartered Accountants for specific implementations. N. Salary Structure Components

1. Basic Salary Components

Earnings

  • Basic Salary (40–60% of CTC)
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA)
  • Dearness Allowance (DA)
  • Conveyance Allowance
  • Medical Allowance
  • Special Allowance
  • Performance Incentives
  • Overtime (if applicable)

Deductions

  • Provident Fund (PF)
  • Employee State Insurance (ESI)
  • Income Tax (TDS)
  • Professional Tax
  • Loan EMIs
  • Advance Recovery
  • Leave Deductions
  • Other Deductions

Statutory Compliance

2. Provident Fund (PF) Calculation

  • Applicability: Organizations with 20+ employees
  • Employee Contribution: 12% of Basic + DA
  • Employer Contribution: 12% of Basic + DA
  • Maximum Limit: ₹15,000 per month (for PF contribution)

Example

  • Basic Salary: ₹25,000; DA: ₹5,000
  • PF Base: ₹15,000 (capped)
  • Employee PF: ₹15,000 × 12% = ₹1,800
  • Employer PF: ₹15,000 × 12% = ₹1,800

3. Employee State Insurance (ESI)

  • Applicability: Employees earning up to ₹21,000 per month
  • Employee Contribution: 0.75% of gross salary
  • Employer Contribution: 3.25% of gross salary

Example

  • Gross Salary: ₹18,000
  • Employee ESI: ₹18,000 × 0.75% = ₹135
  • Employer ESI: ₹18,000 × 3.25% = ₹585

4. Income Tax (TDS) Calculation

Annual approach:

  1. Calculate annual gross salary
  2. Deduct standard deduction (₹50,000)
  3. Deduct Section 80C investments (max ₹1,50,000)
  4. Deduct other allowable deductions
  5. Apply tax slabs
  6. Divide by 12 for monthly TDS

Tax Slabs (New Regime — illustrative)

  • Up to ₹3,00,000: Nil
  • ₹3,00,001 to ₹6,00,000: 5%
  • ₹6,00,001 to ₹9,00,000: 10%
  • ₹9,00,001 to ₹12,00,000: 15%
  • Above ₹12,00,000: 20%/30%

Monthly Statutory Returns

5. PF Returns and Payments

  • ECR (Electronic Challan cum Return): 15th of next month
  • PF Payment: 15th of next month
  • Form 5 (Annual Return): 30th April
  • Form 3A (Annual Accounts): 30th April

6. ESI Returns and Payments

  • Monthly ESI Contribution: 15th of next month
  • Half-yearly Return: January & July
  • Annual Return: 30th April

7. TDS Returns

  • Form 24Q (Quarterly): 31st July, 31st Oct, 31st Jan, 31st May
  • TDS Payment: 7th of next month
  • Form 16 Issue: 15th June

HRA Exemption Calculation

HRA exemption is the least of:

  1. Actual HRA received
  2. 50% of Basic (Metro) / 40% of Basic (Non-Metro)
  3. Actual rent paid minus 10% of Basic

Example: Basic ₹30,000, HRA ₹12,000, Rent ₹15,000 (Metro)

  • Option 1: ₹12,000
  • Option 2: ₹30,000 × 50% = ₹15,000
  • Option 3: ₹15,000 − (₹30,000 × 10%) = ₹12,000
  • HRA Exemption: ₹12,000

Common Compliance Issues

  • Delayed PF/ESI deposits attracting interest and damages
  • Incorrect wage definition for statutory contributions
  • Missing Form 16 / quarterly TDS return deadlines
  • Inadequate documentation for HRA and other exemptions
