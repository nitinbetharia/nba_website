Income Tax Return Filing Checklist

By N. Betharia & Associates · 4 August 2025

Document checklist for ITR filing for Assessment Year 2024-25 (FY 2023-24).

Assessment Year 2024-25 (Financial Year 2023-24)

Important disclaimer

This checklist is for general guidance only. Requirements vary by circumstances and sources of income. Prepared in accordance with the Income Tax Act, 1961 for AY 2024-25. For specific advice, consult a qualified Chartered Accountant. N. Betharia & Associates does not assume responsibility for omissions or errors based solely on this checklist.

Personal Information Documents

  • [ ] PAN Card copy
  • [ ] Aadhaar Card copy
  • [ ] Bank account details (for refund processing)
  • [ ] Current address proof

Income Documents

  • [ ] Form 16 from employer(s)
  • [ ] Salary slips (last month of FY)
  • [ ] Bank interest certificates
  • [ ] Rental income documents (if applicable)
  • [ ] Business income details (P&L, Balance Sheet)
  • [ ] Capital gains statements
  • [ ] Other income documents (dividends, etc.)

Investment & Deduction Proofs

Section 80C Investments (up to ₹1,50,000)

  • [ ] PPF investment receipts
  • [ ] ELSS mutual fund statements
  • [ ] NSC investment certificates
  • [ ] Life insurance premium receipts
  • [ ] Home loan principal repayment certificate
  • [ ] Tax saving fixed deposit receipts
  • [ ] Children’s tuition fee receipts

Other Deductions

  • [ ] Health insurance premium receipts (Section 80D)
  • [ ] Education loan interest certificate (Section 80E)
  • [ ] Donation receipts with 80G certificates
  • [ ] NPS contribution receipts (Section 80CCD)
  • [ ] Home loan interest certificate (Section 24b)

TDS & Advance Tax

  • [ ] TDS certificates (Form 16A, 16B, 16C)
  • [ ] Advance tax payment receipts
  • [ ] SFT (Statement of Financial Transactions) if applicable

Property Related (if applicable)

  • [ ] Property tax receipts
  • [ ] Property maintenance receipts
  • [ ] Rental agreements

Capital Market Transactions

  • [ ] Share trading statements
  • [ ] Mutual fund statements
  • [ ] Dividend income statements

Final Checklist

  • [ ] All income sources properly documented
  • [ ] Tax liability calculated correctly
  • [ ] Return reviewed for errors
  • [ ] Return digitally signed/verified
  • [ ] ITR-V sent to CPC Bangalore (if required)

Important Dates for AY 2024-25

  • Due Date for ITR (Non-Audit): 31st July 2024
  • Due Date for ITR (Audit Cases): 31st October 2024
  • Last Date for ITR-V: 120 days from e-filing

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Incorrect bank account details for refund
  • Not reporting all sources of income
  • Claiming deductions without proper documentation
  • Mathematical errors in calculations
  • Wrong ITR form selection
